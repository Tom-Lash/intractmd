9. Deprescribing Intelligence Surface

IntractMD™ includes a Deprescribing Intelligence surface that uses an AI-powered N+1 counterfactual simulation to identify medications in a patient’s regimen that appear in published deprescribing criteria, including the American Geriatrics Society Beers Criteria® (2023), STOPP/START v3, Canadian Family Physician deprescribing guidelines, and CMS Star Ratings quality measures D09 and D10. Users of this surface acknowledge and agree to the following:

  • Not a recommendation to discontinue medication. All output from the Deprescribing Intelligence surface, including ranked deprescribing candidates, Composite Polypharmacy Risk Score (CPRS) delta estimates, evidence citations, and before/after risk comparisons, constitutes a prompt for pharmacist and prescriber review only. Nothing in the output of this surface constitutes a recommendation, instruction, or authorization to reduce, taper, or discontinue any medication.
  • Prescriber involvement required. No medication should be reduced, tapered, or discontinued based on IntractMD output without the explicit involvement and approval of the patient’s prescribing physician or licensed healthcare provider. Deprescribing without appropriate medical supervision can cause serious harm, including withdrawal syndromes, disease relapse, and cardiovascular events.
  • Population-level criteria. Beers Criteria, STOPP/START, and other evidence sources incorporated into the Deprescribing Intelligence surface represent population-level guidelines developed for general clinical guidance. These criteria do not account for individual patient circumstances, comorbidities, contraindications, or clinical history. A medication flagged by these criteria may be entirely appropriate for a specific patient.
  • CPRS delta scores are estimates. The Composite Polypharmacy Risk Score and the estimated score change associated with each deprescribing candidate are algorithmic estimates based on structured interaction data. They are not validated clinical predictions and should not be used as the sole basis for any clinical decision.
  • For institutional and clinical use. The Deprescribing Intelligence surface is designed for use by licensed pharmacists, physicians, and qualified healthcare professionals operating within their scope of practice. It is not intended for unsupervised patient self-use.
  • Excluded medications. The platform excludes certain medication classes from deprescribing candidacy, including medications identified as rescue therapy, replacement therapy, or life-sustaining treatment. These exclusions are based on published criteria and may not capture every clinical scenario. Users should apply independent clinical judgment.
